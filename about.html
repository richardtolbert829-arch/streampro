About us

The people behind the plain-language guides that help you get your TV streaming.

We believe setting up a TV should take minutes, not evenings — and that nobody should feel lost staring at an activation code.

Brightline TV began with a simple frustration. New TVs and streaming devices are brilliant, but the first-time setup — app stores, sign-in screens, codes and Wi-Fi settings — leaves many people unsure what to do next.

So we started writing the kind of guides we wished existed: short, clear, honest and free.

Today we combine those guides with a small, friendly support team that answers questions six days a week.

We're fully independent and not affiliated with any TV manufacturer or streaming provider. That means our only job is to help you get set up — safely, and without any sales pressure.
