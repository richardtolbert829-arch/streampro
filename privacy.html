Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and look after your information.

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Brightline TV (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses and protects information when you visit our website or contact us.

01

Information we collect

  • What you tell us: your name, email address, device type and message when you contact us.
  • Technical data: browser type, device type, pages visited and approximate location from your IP address.
  • Cookies: small files that keep the site working and, with your consent, help us understand usage.

We do not collect streaming account passwords, payment card numbers, bank details or one-time security codes.

02

How we use it

  • To answer your questions and support requests
  • To run, maintain and improve the website
  • To protect against fraud, abuse and security risks
  • To meet our legal obligations
03

Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent personal information. We may share limited data with service providers — such as hosting, email and analytics — who work on our behalf under confidentiality obligations, or when the law requires it.

04

Cookies

You can control or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some parts of the site may not work properly without them.

05

How long we keep it

We keep messages only for as long as needed to help you and for reasonable record-keeping, then delete or anonymize them.

06

Keeping it safe

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, although no online transmission is ever completely secure.

07

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or object to how we use it. Email care@brightlinetv.netlify.app to make a request.

08

Children

This website isn't intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information.

09

Other websites

Our guides may link to official websites of streaming services and device makers. Their own privacy policies apply there.

10

Updates to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of the page shows the latest revision.

11

Contact

Write to us at care@brightlinetv.netlify.app or 789 Placeholder Road, Unit 5, Your City, ST 00000.

In short

  • We only collect what we need to help you
  • We never ask for passwords or card details
  • We don't sell your personal information
  • You can ask us to access or delete your data

Questions? Email care@brightlinetv.netlify.app
