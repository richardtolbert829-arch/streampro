How it works

Five easy steps, every time.

Almost every streaming app uses the same activation process. Learn it once and you can set up any app on any screen.

Stuck on a step?
01

Download the app

Find the streaming app in your TV or device's app store and install it.

02

Choose “Sign in”

Open the app and pick the sign-in or activate option. A short code appears on screen.

03

Open the official site

On your phone or computer, go to the activation address shown on your TV — always the provider's official website.

04

Enter the code

Type the code exactly as shown and sign in to your account with the provider.

05

Press play

Your TV refreshes within seconds. That's it — you're ready to watch.

FAQ

Good questions, straight answers.

Q1Is activating a streaming app free?

Yes. Activation through the provider's official website costs nothing — you just need an active account with that service. Be cautious of anyone asking you to pay to activate.

Q2Where exactly do I type the code?

Your TV shows the web address to visit. Open it on your phone or computer, check it's the provider's official site, then enter the code there.

Q3Why did my code stop working?

Activation codes expire after a few minutes. Go back in the TV app and choose Sign in again to get a fresh one.

Q4I entered the code but my TV didn't change.

Wait about 30 seconds, then close and reopen the app. If it still won't sign in, check your connection, update the app and restart the device.

Q5Are you the official support for my TV or app?

No — we're an independent help website. For billing or account problems, please contact your provider's official support team.

Q6How quickly will you reply to me?

We aim to answer every message within one business day, Monday to Saturday.
